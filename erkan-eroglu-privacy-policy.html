01

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Word & Logic, developed by Erkan Eroglu, handles information when you use the mobile application or visit this website. We believe your data belongs to you and have designed the app with privacy in mind.

02

Data Collection

Word & Logic does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information. The app does not transmit your game activity, progress, scores, preferences, or device identifiers to our servers.

No account. No personal profile. No server-side game history.

03

Data Stored on Your Device

Game progress, high scores, and app preferences are stored locally on your device so the app can remember your experience. This information remains under your control and is not sent to us.

Removing the app may permanently delete this locally stored information. Depending on your device settings, your operating system or backup provider may manage device backups independently under its own privacy policy.

04

Children's Privacy

Word & Logic does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. If you believe personal information has been provided to us in error, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

05

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, legal requirements, or our privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

06

Contact

If you have a question or concern about this Privacy Policy or Word & Logic's privacy practices, please contact the developer, Erkan Eroglu, through the official app support channel.
